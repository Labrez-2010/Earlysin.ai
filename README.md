# Earlysin.ai 

### Ai powered monitering system 
A system that use a ai and smart wearable to moniter a real time health tracking , ai health analysis , emergency alert , caregiver connectivity , and predictive healthcare insights 


**the platform combines** : 

- smart wearable divice intergration 
- Ai powered health assistant 
- web app support and health dashboard 
- Emergency monitering system
- caregiver connectivity 
- predictive health analytics

### Features

**Real time health monitering**

- Heart rate tracking 
- Blood oxygen (Spo2) monitering 
- Body temperature tracking 
- Sleep monitoring 
- stress level detection
- Activity & step tracking 
- Fall detection 
- Abnormal health patterns detection

**Ai Health assistant** 

- Ai powered health insights
- personalized health Recommendations 
- Smart alerts for unusual body conditions
- Symptom analysis 
- health trend prediction 
- Daily Heath reports 
- preventive Healthcare suggestions 

**Caregiver & Family monitering**

- health dashboard for user parent / caregiver 
- Emergency alert to connected parent / caregiver
- real tome location sharing 
- health notification 
- safty alert for students & elderly user 


**Emergency System**

- Automatic Emergency alert 
- abnormal heatbeat Detection 
- health risk prediction 
- Emergency Connect 
- location based emergency assistance 

**Analytics Dashboard**
- Real-time health charts 
- Weekly & monthly health Report 
- Activity analytics 
- Sleep analysis
- previous health data tracking 
- full report of user under one screen
